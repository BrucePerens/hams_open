User Websites Portal Feature Documentation

Creating Your Site

Every user is entitled to a personal website. To get started:

  1. Open your site at hams.com/your-callsign (your website name), or choose My Website in the menu.
  2. If your home page isn't set up yet, you'll see a "Create Your Home" button.
  3. Clicking this button creates your home page with a default layout.
  4. Use the owner toolbar across the top of your site (see below) to write your home page and add more pages and posts.

Blogging & Subscriptions

Share your stories and updates with the community through your personal blog.

  • Access your blog at /your-username/blog.
  • Initialize it by clicking "Create Your Blog".
  • New posts can be created directly from the blog index page when you are logged in.
  • Subscriptions: Users can subscribe to your site to receive email notifications when you post new content.

Editing and Publishing a Post

A fresh "New Post" starts out titled exactly that, with an empty body and unpublished. To give it a real title, write its body, and publish it:

  1. From your blog index page, click "Edit" on the post (this is also where "New Post" sends you right after creating one).
  2. Change the title and/or body, check "Published" when you're ready for others to see it, and click "Save".

This dedicated edit form exists because the generic website-builder "Edit" toolbar does not render for personal site owners -- it requires a sitewide website-editing permission that is not scoped to just your own content.

Your Site Tools: Toolbar, Dashboard and Editor

Your site is its own small website at hams.com/your-callsign, with your name and menu on top and a plain footer. Only you see the tools described here.

The owner toolbar

When you are signed in and look at any page of your own site, a dark bar appears above the site menu:

  • Manage site: your dashboard.
  • New page and New blog post: open the editor on something new.
  • Edit this page / Edit this post: edit what you are looking at.
  • Images, Privacy: your pictures, and your data export and erasure page.
  • Publish this page / Unpublish this page.
  • View as visitor: hides the bar so you can see the site as everyone else does. Reload without ?visitor=1 to bring it back.

The dashboard and the page editor

Choose Manage site (or My Website in the main menu) to list your pages, blog posts and images, with Edit, Publish and Unpublish for each. Nothing is deleted: Unpublish hides a page or post from visitors and keeps it, and you can publish it again at any time.

The editor

You write in the page itself, as you would in a word processor, and never see any code. The toolbar above the writing area has headings, bold, italic, underline, inline code, bulleted and numbered lists, quotes, code blocks, left/center/right alignment, links, pictures, undo and redo. Text you paste is cleaned of other programs' fonts and colours. Every button has a text label for screen readers, and the writing area can be reached with the Tab key.

  • Links start with http://, https://, mailto: or /.
  • Pictures are JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP files of at most 2 MB, up to 100 per site. Pictures hosted elsewhere cannot be used.
  • A page has a title, an address (/your-callsign/address; letters, digits and hyphens, fixed once the page exists) and a Published box. Addresses such as home and blog are used by the site itself and cannot be chosen.
  • Your pages appear in your site's menu once published.

Everything you save is checked by the server. Scripts, frames and similar are removed, and an attempt to include them is reported to the moderators like any other violation.

Your address

Your home page is hams.com/your-callsign. Older links of the form hams.com/your-callsign/home still work: they are redirected.

Community Directory

The Community Directory is where you can discover websites created by others.

  • To appear in the directory, go to your Settings and check "Show in Public Directory".
  • The directory shows the total view count for each site, giving you an idea of what's popular.

Total View Aggregation

The Community Directory now displays the total view count for each website. This count is aggregated from all public pages and blog posts owned by the user or group. For maximum platform performance, these counters are updated in batches using high-performance database procedures.

Content Reporting & Safety

We are committed to maintaining a safe environment for everyone.

  • Report Violation: If you find any content that is inappropriate or violates our guidelines, click the "Report Violation" button at the bottom of the page.
  • Sending a report: the form checks that you are a person with a small calculation your browser does by itself, with nothing to click and no third party involved. You can keep typing while it runs. If your browser cannot do it, a link opens a page with one question in words. A visitor who is not signed in gives an email address and describes the problem in at least ten characters, with no more than two links; a signed-in member just describes it. If the check says you sent too many reports, wait an hour.
  • Moderation: Our team reviews all reports. Users who repeatedly violate guidelines may receive strikes. After 3 strikes, website features will be suspended.

Account Appeals

If your account has been suspended, you can submit a one-time appeal through your Portal Dashboard. Explain clearly why you believe the suspension should be lifted.

Your Data & Privacy (GDPR, the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation)

You have full control over your data:

  • Data Export: Download all your pages and posts in JSON (JavaScript Object Notation, a standard machine-readable data format) for your own records.
  • Right to Erasure: Permanently delete all your content from our system. Warning: This action is irreversible.

Administration & Moderation (Admins Only)

For administrators managing the platform, the following UI features are available:

  • Global Settings: Navigate to Settings > General Settings > User Websites to configure the global page limit (the default maximum number of pages each user may create, 100 unless changed) and designate platform administrators.
  • Moderation Dashboard: Access the User Websites app from the main Odoo dashboard to review pending Violation Reports. Admins can mark reports as Under Review, Dismiss them, or Take Action & Strike the offending user.
  • Appeals Management: Review appeals submitted by suspended users. Admins can read the user's reasoning and either Approve (pardon) or Reject the appeal.
  • Toast Notifications: Admins receive real-time toast notifications (brief pop-up messages) in the Odoo backend when new violation reports are submitted, ensuring rapid response times.
