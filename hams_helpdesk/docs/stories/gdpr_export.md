# Story: A Member's Data Export Includes Their Helpdesk Tickets

* **Story:** As a member, I want my data export to include the helpdesk tickets I filed. `res.users._get_gdpr_export_data` adds `helpdesk_tickets` (subject, description, callsign, type, stage, creation time) for tickets whose customer is my partner, read through the GDPR service account, so another member's ticket is never exported. Staff notes, triage fields and the thread are not included, and neither is a child-safety report (type csam_enticement_trafficking, or one naming a reported user or recording): those are held for the law and never handed to the account they concern. Erasure of tickets is an open policy question and is not done here -> [@ANCHOR: hams_helpdesk:res_users_get_gdpr_export_data]; checked by [@ANCHOR: hams_helpdesk:test_gdpr_export_is_own_tickets_only].
