# VNA limits

In progress. The ranges of every argument are in the argument tables of the tool pages ([bench_vna_sweep](tools/bench_vna_sweep) and the other `bench_vna_*` tools). Until this page is written: sweep values are raw, uncalibrated ratios; the frequency meter's sign and absolute accuracy are not verified against a standard; only one AM setting (500 Hz, 100 %) is decoded; there is no spectrum-analyzer tool.
