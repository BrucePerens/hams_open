## Events and explanations

- `unsteady_tone` x4, all at the 100 kHz first point: the known first-point settling
  retry (about 0.03 % of attempts); the retry cured each one and no wrong value was returned.
- `usb_transfer_error` x1, round 206, 2026-10-29 23:25:04, point 30.588 MHz: read request
  0x0d returned a pipe error on seven consecutive tries with growing back-off (6 to 161 ms),
  while the preceding writes (0x40 to 0x64) were all ok and no audio had been analysed.
  The attempt was abandoned, the next attempt succeeded, the point was correct. The kernel
  journal for the window is empty (no xHCI reset, no disconnect). Cause: **not explained**;
  a transient stall of the read endpoint that the bounded retry handled. Evidence kept in
  `events.jsonl`. One in about 400 rounds; watch the rate in the next soak.
- `usb_topology_changed` x5: false alarms. Bruce unplugged and replugged his USB-C dock
  (webcam, keyboard and mouse hubs, r8152 Ethernet). The VNWA subtree (3-2.2) was unchanged.
  The harness compares the whole bus; to-do filed to compare only the unit's subtree.
- `scheduled_power_cycle` x6: all OK, about 14 s to re-enumerate and about 13 s to the
  first good sweep.
- End of run: site power loss. Not a unit fault.
