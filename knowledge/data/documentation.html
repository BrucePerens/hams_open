Welcome to Manuals

Modern. Simple. Open Source.
This guide will show you how to use Manuals to manage documentation effectively.

1. Getting Started

Manuals is your central hub for all types of documentation. From internal policies to public help guides, everything is organized in a clear, hierarchical structure that makes finding information effortless.

2. Finding Your Way Around

We provide multiple tools to help you navigate through deep documentation:

  • The Sidebar: On the left side of the manual portal, you'll find the full tree of articles. Workspace articles have an Internal Permission of "Read Only" or "Read & Write". Shared articles are "No Access" articles that have Shared Members (you are one of them, or you created the article), and Private articles are "No Access" articles you created without any Shared Members. Visitors who are not staff can only open published articles and articles shared with them.
  • Search: Use the search bar at the top of the sidebar to find keywords in titles or the body of articles. It matches any part of a title or body, ignoring upper and lower case.
  • Breadcrumbs: At the top of every page, the breadcrumb trail (e.g., Manuals > Guides > Setup) shows you exactly where you are and lets you jump back to parent folders.
  • Table of Contents: On articles that contain H2 or H3 headings, a "On this page" menu appears on the right (on wide screens), allowing you to jump directly to specific sections.

3. Creating Your First Article

Writing documentation should be as easy as writing an email. To start:

  1. Go to the Manuals app in your Odoo dashboard.
  2. Click New. Creating and editing articles requires the Manual Administrator group; other users can read articles but not change them.
  3. Add a Title and an Icon (like an emoji) to help users identify the topic.
  4. Use the Rich Text Editor to add content. Use Headings (H2, H3) to structure your text and automatically generate the Table of Contents.
  5. Choose a Parent Article if you want to file this article inside an existing folder.
  6. Author Attribution: The system automatically tracks who created or last updated an article and displays it to readers.
  7. Markdown Support: If you paste raw Markdown (like ## Headers or **bold**), the system automatically detects and compiles it on the frontend! This only happens for plain paragraphs of text: if the article already contains headings, lists, tables, images or similar formatting, it is shown as written.

4. Sharing and Security

You have full control over who sees your documentation:

  • Public Access: Click the Is Published button in the top right of the article form to make it visible to anyone on the internet at /manual.
  • Internal Permissions: Use the Internal Permission setting to choose between "Read Only", "Read & Write" (collaborative), or "No Access" (private). "Read Only" lets all staff read the article. "Read & Write" is meant for shared editing, but in this release only Manual Administrators can edit articles, so it currently works like "Read Only". "No Access" limits the article to its creator, its Shared Members and Manual Administrators.
  • Shared Members: If an article is private, you can still share it with specific people by adding them to the Shared Members list. The list appears on the article form when Internal Permission is "No Access".

5. Multi-Website and Company Support

If your Odoo handles multiple websites or companies, Manuals keeps things organized. You can assign articles to a specific Website or Company, ensuring that users only see the documentation relevant to their current context. The company setting applies everywhere. The website setting controls which website's sidebar, search results and name links show the article.

6. Giving Feedback

Help us improve! At the bottom of every public article, you'll find buttons to vote if the article was helpful. Each vote adds one to the article's helpful or unhelpful count with a single database update, so votes arriving at the same moment are all counted. Only the counts are stored, not who voted, and each browser session can vote once per article. This helps documentation authors know which guides need more work.

7. Quick Tips

  • Organize by Topic: Use parent articles as folders (e.g., "HR Policies", "Technical Guides").
  • Use Images: A screenshot is often better than a paragraph of text.
  • Links: You can link between articles to create a seamless knowledge web.
  • Direct Name Links: You can share an article instantly by its exact name using the URL /manual/by_name/Your+Article+Name. Upper and lower case do not matter, but the rest of the name must match exactly.
